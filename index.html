About the Workshop

Paint Your Own Sculpture

Join artist Swati Pasari for an afternoon of painting at CCA, Bikaner House. In this hands-on session, each participant will paint on a fiber-made bud-like sculpture, bringing it to life with colour, texture, and personal expression.

No prior experience is needed. All materials will be provided, and you will take your finished sculpture home at the end of the session.

All materials provided: Paints, brushes, and the fiber sculpture will be ready for you at the venue. Just bring yourself and your creativity.

Please note: Arrive 10–15 minutes early for a smooth start. Seats are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

About the Artist

Swati Pasari

Swati Pasari is a multidisciplinary artist whose vibrant work spans painting, sculpture, and mixed media. Her practice explores colour, form, and the interplay between organic shapes and bold expression.

Through her workshops, Swati invites participants to discover their own creative voice, guiding them through intuitive mark-making and the joy of working with colour on three-dimensional forms.
